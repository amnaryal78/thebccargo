5 Common Customs Clearance Mistakes Nepal Importers Make (And How to Avoid Them)

RI
Ramesh Aryal
IT & Systems Lead

Customs bottlenecks can severely impact supply chain timelines and inflate import tariffs. Working with experienced, certified customs brokers eliminates these pitfalls before shipments depart origin ports.

The Top 5 Customs Pitfalls:

  1. Misclassified HS tariff codes leading to incorrect duty assessments
  2. Incomplete commercial invoices lacking itemized unit values
  3. Missing Certificate of Origin (CO/SAFTA) required for tariff concessions
  4. Failure to declare restricted or hazardous goods in advance
  5. Inaccurate consignee PAN/Vat tax registration details
