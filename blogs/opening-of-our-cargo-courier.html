Grand Opening of BC Cargo & Courier Service

We are excited to announce the official Grand Opening of BC Cargo & Courier Service on Bhadra 1, 2083 (August 17, 2026). This marks the beginning of a new journey dedicated to providing reliable, secure, and efficient logistics solutions for individuals and businesses across Nepal and beyond.

Our mission is simple: Connecting Nepal to the World. Whether you need to send parcels within the country or ship internationally, BC Cargo is committed to delivering your packages safely and on time.

Our Services

  • Domestic Cargo Services
  • International Courier
  • Express Delivery
  • Door-to-Door Delivery

Why Choose BC Cargo?

  • Fast and reliable delivery
  • Safe handling of shipments
  • Competitive pricing
  • Professional customer support
  • Nationwide and international coverage

We sincerely thank everyone who has supported us throughout this journey. We look forward to serving our valued customers with excellence and building lasting relationships based on trust and reliability.

Join us as we celebrate this exciting milestone and experience a new standard in cargo and courier services.
