Our commitment to transparency, data protection, cargo safety, and fair trade practices for global shippers.

Privacy Policy

At THE BC Cargo & Courier (part of THE BC NETWORKS), protecting your privacy and business telemetry is a fundamental operational principle. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and safeguard data when you use our website, cargo portal, or freight services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect essential operational data required for logistics booking and customs declarations:

  • Identity & Contact Details: Full name, company affiliation, email address, telephone/WhatsApp contact details.
  • Shipment Telemetry: Origin/destination addresses, cargo weight, commercial invoices, and customs paperwork.
  • Digital Telemetry: IP address, device telemetry, browser type, and cookie preferences for client portal authentication.

2. How We Use Data

Your data is exclusively processed to perform shipping contracts, provide live tracking updates, process payments, and fulfill statutory customs obligations. We never sell your personal or shipping information to third-party advertisers.
