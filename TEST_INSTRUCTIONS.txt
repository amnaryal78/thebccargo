          THE BC CARGO & COURIER - EMAIL & LEAD SYSTEM TEST INSTRUCTIONS
================================================================================

WHY EMAILS WERE NOT BEING RECEIVED:
----------------------------------
1. The project was missing a `backend/.env` file containing real SMTP email credentials
   (SMTP_USER, SMTP_PASS). Without valid SMTP credentials, Nodemailer skipped 
   sending real emails and logged a diagnostic warning.
2. The security middleware in `server.js` was rejecting non-GET API requests 
   originating from certain origins with a 403 Forbidden status.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE TO CONFIGURE & TEST REAL EMAIL DELIVERY:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: Configure Your Real Email Credentials
---------------------------------------------
1. Open the file `backend/.env` in your editor.
2. If using Gmail or Google Workspace:
   a. Go to your Google Account: https://myaccount.google.com/
   b. Turn ON 2-Step Verification: https://myaccount.google.com/signinoptions/two-step-verification
   c. Generate a 16-character App Password: https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
      (Select App: "Mail", Device: "Other (BC Cargo)")
3. Update `backend/.env` with your email and generated 16-character App Password:
   
   SMTP_HOST=smtp.gmail.com
   SMTP_PORT=587
   SMTP_USER=your_email@gmail.com
   SMTP_PASS=xxxx xxxx xxxx xxxx
   ADMIN_EMAIL=your_email@gmail.com


STEP 2: Run the Diagnostic Test Tool
------------------------------------
Open a terminal in the project directory and run:

   node backend/test-email.js

- If successful, you will see:
  "✅ SMTP Connection & Authentication Successful!"
  "✅ Test Email Sent Successfully!"
- Check your inbox (and Spam/Junk folder) for the test email.


STEP 3: Start the Backend Server
--------------------------------
Run:

   cd backend
   npm start

The server will launch on port 3000.


STEP 4: Test Website Form Submissions
-------------------------------------
1. Open `contact.html` in your browser.
   - Fill in: Full Name, Email, Phone, Service, Message.
   - Click "Send Freight Inquiry".
   - Verify green success notification banner appears.
   - Check your administrator inbox for the new formatted email alert!

2. Open `careers.html` in your browser.
   - Click "Submit General Resume".
   - Fill in: Name, Email, Phone, Position, Experience, Cover Note.
   - Click "Submit Application".
   - Verify success modal appears and email alert is sent!

3. Open `become-partner.html` in your browser.
   - Fill in: Company Name, Country, First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, Details.
   - Click "Submit Application".
   - Verify success banner appears and email alert is sent!


STEP 5: Test Admin Panel & Direct Reply
---------------------------------------
1. Navigate to: `http://localhost:3000/bc-mgmt-x9k2p7`
   - Automatically sets admin authorization and redirects to `/hq-access`.
2. Log in with admin credentials:
   - Username: admin
   - Password: BcCargo@2026!
3. Go to "Form Submissions" in the sidebar menu (or click the top red notification bell).
   - Click the "Eye" icon on any lead to open full details.
   - Notice that the lead's "NEW" badge disappears and unread counter decreases by 1.
4. Click "Reply via Email" inside the modal.
   - Type a response to the customer/applicant and click "Send Email Reply".
   - Verify the toast notification appears and the customer receives your email!
